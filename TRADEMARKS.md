# Trademarks and brand use

**The code is MIT. The brand is not.**

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) grants broad rights to use, copy, modify, and
redistribute the *software*. It does **not** grant any right to use the
**1132 Fixer** name, logo, or icons. Those are project marks, and this document
explains what use of them is and is not permitted.

## Marks covered

- The name **“1132 Fixer”** and **“1132 Fixer for Windows.”**
- The 1132 Fixer **logo** and wordmark.
- The application **icons** shipped under `assets/` (for example
  `assets/icon.ico`, `assets/icon.png`, `assets/1132-fixer-logo-transparent.png`,
  `assets/1132-helper-shortcut.ico`).

## What you may do

- Use, modify, and redistribute the source code under the terms of the MIT
  licence.
- State factually that your work is **“based on 1132 Fixer”** or **“a fork of
  1132 Fixer,”** as long as that statement does not imply endorsement or that
  your build is the official one.
- Keep the existing marks in an **unmodified** build that you redistribute
  without changes, where doing so does not misrepresent the source.

## What you may not do

- Use the **1132 Fixer name, logo, or icons** as the name or branding of a
  **modified** build, fork, or derivative in a way that implies it is the
  official 1132 Fixer product or is endorsed by or affiliated with the project.
- Imply endorsement, affiliation, partnership, or an official relationship where
  none exists.
- Use the marks for a different product, or in a way that is misleading, or that
  suggests your distribution is the canonical download.
- Register the marks, or confusingly similar marks, as your own.

## If you fork

If you distribute a modified build, please **use your own name and icons** for
it. You are welcome to acknowledge the origin — for example, “forked from
[1132 Fixer for Windows](https://github.com/1132-Fixer/windows)” — but present it
as your own product, not as 1132 Fixer.
