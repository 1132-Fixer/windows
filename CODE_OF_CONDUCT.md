# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

1132 Fixer is a small, practical project. We want taking part — filing an issue,
asking for help, or sending a pull request — to be a respectful experience for
everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be precise and be kind. Assume good faith.
- Keep discussion focused on the software and the problem at hand.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect that maintainers are volunteers and triage takes time.
- Redact personal data (usernames, SIDs, profile paths, secrets) before pasting
  logs or support reports into a public issue.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, or unwelcome personal attention.
- Publishing others’ private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately posting another person’s secrets, credentials, or unredacted
  personal data.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or other conduct that a reasonable person
  would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions, and any project communication channel — and when an
individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainers:

- Use the contact path on [1132-fixer.xyz](https://1132-fixer.xyz/) with
  **Code of Conduct** in the subject, or
- For conduct concerns tied to a security-sensitive report, use
  [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/1132-Fixer/windows/security/advisories/new).

Reports are handled confidentially. Do not use public issues to report a Code of
Conduct concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
They may take any action they judge appropriate, including editing or removing
comments, commits, issues, and pull requests, and temporarily or permanently
banning any contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening,
or harmful. Enforcement decisions are made in good faith and applied
consistently.
